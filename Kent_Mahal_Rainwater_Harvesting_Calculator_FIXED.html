5. Engineering assumptions

  • Assumed roof inclination: 35°–40°.
  • For vertically falling rain, use the horizontal projected roof catchment area, not the longer sloping roof surface. Roof pitch does not increase the rainfall volume captured.
  • 90% collection efficiency is an illustrative assumption. Actual efficiency depends on roof material, gutters, downpipes, leakage, first-flush diversion and maintenance.
  • 15 mm/day is an assumed monsoon average. Seasonal yield requires local rainfall records and the number of effective rainy days.

Indicative planning estimate only; confirm actual areas and hydraulic design on site.
